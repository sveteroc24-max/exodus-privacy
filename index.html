Privacy Policy

Exodus: Arrow Escape Puzzle · Last updated 23 September 2026

Who we are

Exodus: Arrow Escape Puzzle (“the Game”) is developed by Svetlana. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at sveteroc24@gmail.com.

Information the Game collects

The Game itself does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no registration and no login. Your progress, coins, purchased items and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

Information collected by advertising

The Game shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads are never forced: they are shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for coins, extra moves, or to unlock a level.

To serve these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • information about ads shown to you and your interaction with them.

This data is processed by Google, not by us. We never receive it and have no access to it. Google’s handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your device settings, under Settings → Google → Ads. The Game remains fully playable if you never watch a single ad — all content can be unlocked by playing.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Permissions

The Game requests internet access, used only to load advertising, and access to the advertising identifier, used by Google for the purposes described above. No other permissions are requested.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.
